# HCI-SI 2026 supplementary evidence update — v1.1

This package adds the **Round 14 codebook/evidence verification** to the pre-existing v1.0 51-publication dataset. It does **not** replace the article's search strategy, included studies, or original research questions.

## Authoritative workbooks
- `data/master_review_analysis_workbook_FINAL_RECHECKED.xlsx`: previously released 51-study analytical workbook (unchanged copy).
- `verification/round14_final_codebook_review.xlsx`: latest supplied codebook-reconciliation and source-evidence audit. **The stricter implemented-only and measured-only frequencies remain provisional**, not publication-ready independently confirmed counts.

## Historical audit trail (optional)
`verification/archive/` holds intermediate rounds 7, 11 and 13. These are history, not alternative final results. Do not add historical claim tallies across files: rounds overlap and claims require publication-family deduplication.

## Interpretation
- Tables 6 and 7 in the manuscript count literature-presence categories, not proven implementations or measured outcomes.
- The broad usability family count (37) is not the number of studies administering the standard ten-item SUS; the Round 14 audit documents three such publications.
- Some adjudications are qualified by phase, construct, original reporting, and source availability; `not established` must not be interpreted as proven absent.
- The audit uses article identifiers and internal claim labels. The original study-level source and license rights should be checked before publication.

## Publication and privacy review REQUIRED
Before uploading to a public repository, the author must check each workbook for (i) copyrighted verbatim passages/excerpts, (ii) personal or sensitive information and workbook comments, (iii) misleading unreviewed or preliminary classifications, and (iv) external links or embedded objects. This package is **not** a certified anonymised or copyright-cleared public release. The files are unchanged copies.

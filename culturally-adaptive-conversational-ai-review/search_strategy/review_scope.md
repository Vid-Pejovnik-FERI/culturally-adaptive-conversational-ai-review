# Review Scope and Study Selection

## PCC framework

**Population / unit of analysis:** chatbots and other conversational AI systems.  
**Concept:** cultural adaptivity and closely related constructs, including cultural awareness, sensitivity, responsiveness, alignment, and appropriateness.  
**Context:** any application domain in which conversational AI is used.

## Main inclusion criteria

- Focuses on a chatbot or another conversational AI system.
- Explicitly addresses a cultural dimension in design, implementation, personalisation, alignment, adaptation, or evaluation.
- Reports information relevant to at least 1 review question.
- Journal article, review article, or conference/proceedings paper.
- English language.
- Published from 2021–2026.
- At least 2 citations under the practical search limits used for the project.

## Main exclusion criteria

- No conversational AI system.
- Culture mentioned only superficially and not linked to design, implementation, or evaluation.
- Non-conversational AI only.
- General theoretical discussion without a chatbot/conversational-AI link.
- Editorial, note, letter, thesis, dissertation, poster, book review, or other non-target publication type.
- Insufficient information for screening.

## Interpretation

The audit distinguishes the presence of mechanisms and metric categories in published literature from evidence of their **implementation or measurement**. Strict implemented-only and measured-only frequencies are **not yet fully adjudicated** and should not be presented as definitive.

The broad SUS/usability coded family contains 37 publications; the subsequent instrument-level audit confirmed three publications with the standard ten-item SUS.
